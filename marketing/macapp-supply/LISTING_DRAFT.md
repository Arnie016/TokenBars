## Public Description

### TOKENBAR

TokenBar is a local-first Mac app that turns aggregate evidence from your
Codex sessions into a five-chapter Builder Story. See how you steer agents,
which patterns keep carrying your work, and the builder identity your sessions
have earned.

### YOUR BUILDER STORY

Move through a narrative built from your own activity: identity, strongest
signal, repeated moves, surprising evidence, and the next edge worth building.
The important metrics stay large, readable, and connected to an explanation.

### PROOF, NOT A PERSONALITY QUIZ

Every title is grounded in inspectable local evidence. Open a signal to see why
it scored, then compare reports over time instead of accepting a generic label.

### SHARE ON YOUR TERMS

Raw prompts, source code, and credentials stay private. TokenBar only creates a
public Builder Identity when you deliberately publish a generated profile.

### REPORTS THAT REMEMBER

Save each Builder Story as an edition. Revisit how your working style changed,
export a proof packet, or share the report that represents you best.

## Brand Directions

1. **Loom Sigil:** Most ownable and closest to the current product metaphor.
2. **Atlas Compass:** Best for discovery and progression; risks looking like navigation.
3. **Colossus Gate:** Strongest mythic identity; too detailed for a small menu-bar icon.
4. **Corsair Ledger:** Most adventurous; risks implying crypto or finance.

Recommendation for the next review: simplify the Loom Sigil into a production
icon while retaining one woven copper strand, one jade strand, and one token
node. Use the Colossus Gate as report-cover art rather than the app icon.
