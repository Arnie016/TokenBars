# TokenBar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 June 2026

TokenBar is a developer tool for private AI coding workflow analytics, builder profiles, weekly AI overviews, and PDF reports.

## Information we collect

TokenBar may collect:

- Email address and payment details through Stripe checkout.
- Basic account and subscription status from Stripe.
- Workflow metadata you explicitly choose to submit for report generation, such as tool usage, session timing, token counts, prompt patterns, repository names, and derived productivity/profile signals.
- Support messages you send to us.

TokenBar is designed so source code does not need to leave your machine. Do not upload secrets, private keys, passwords, proprietary source code, or sensitive personal information.

## Local analysis

TokenBar may analyze coding-agent logs and local workflow files on your machine when you run it. Local analysis is intended to produce a redacted summary or report payload. You should review what is sent before submitting it for hosted report generation.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Provide TokenBar Pro subscriptions and reports.
- Generate AI builder profiles, weekly overviews, and PDF reports.
- Send product, billing, and support emails.
- Prevent abuse, fraud, and payment disputes.
- Improve product reliability and user experience.

## Payment processing

Payments are processed by Stripe. TokenBar does not store full card numbers. Stripe's handling of payment data is governed by Stripe's own terms and privacy policy.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers needed to operate the product, such as payment processing, email delivery, hosting, logging, and AI/report generation providers.

## Data retention and deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records. You may request deletion of your report data or account information by contacting support.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No system is perfectly secure, so you should avoid submitting secrets or highly sensitive data.
