# TokenBar founder voice notes

Status: HUMAN_EDIT_REQUIRED. These are raw founder notes, not final Devpost copy.

Arnav finds inspiring builders and workflows on X, saves discoveries to WhatsApp, and finds the process fragmented. He spends thousands of dollars using model tools but terminal token/cost output has poor presentation and no reflection. He wants beautiful data storytelling about where the money and attention went, a builder identity that reveals taste, ambition, technical ability and discernment, and a social surface where he can discover directions unlike his own instead of building in a vacuum.

Product correction: TokenBar is not token accounting or a generic analytics dashboard. The Create Identity flow exists to convert deliberately selected, safe local evidence into a privacy-aware passport without sharing raw prompts, transcripts, source, secrets, or private paths.

Human decisions to preserve in the eventual draft: which evidence is meaningful, which details are withheld, the visual taste of the passport/share card, and the decision to reject generic public feeds or rankings before this proof journey is genuinely tested.
